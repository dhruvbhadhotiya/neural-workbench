How to use this module

offline · eight labs · one exam

Read badges first

Exact math means the browser computes every number. Illustrative means a stated simulation or stored schematic — two of the eight labs are honest about that.

A toolkit, not a theory module

Each lesson answers an operational question: what do I log, what fits in memory, what moved the prediction, how do I ship it, and what do I check when it breaks?

Same notation throughout

\(\eta\) is the learning rate, \(\lambda\) the decay strength, images are NCHW, and logits are pre-softmax outputs. M6 uses exactly the symbols from M0–M5.
