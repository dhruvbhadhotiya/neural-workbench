Exact math means the number is computed from the pixels or grids on screen. Illustrative means the data is synthetic and the note says so.

From M1 to M5

You already know convolutions and receptive fields. Here the output keeps \(H,W\), so upsampling, skips and sub-pixel alignment become first-class design problems.

Notation is fixed

Images are \((N,C,H,W)\). Dilation is \(D\), kernel \(K\), stride \(S\). IoU and Dice have the same definitions here as in the metrics sections of earlier modules.
