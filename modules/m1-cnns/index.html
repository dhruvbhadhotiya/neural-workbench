How to use this module

offline · exact labs · read in order

Double-click a lesson

Everything runs from file://. No server, no CDN, no downloads. KaTeX is vendored locally and every visual is drawn from numbers computed in your browser.

Read the badge first

Exact math means the numbers come from a computation in the page. All sixteen M1 labs are exact; schematic figures in prose say so.

Notation is fixed

Images are NCHW, conv weights are \((C_{out},C_{in},K_H,K_W)\), logits are pre-softmax outputs, and \(H'\) always follows the one output-size formula.
