How to use this module

offline · exact and illustrative labs · read in order

Double-click a lesson

Everything runs from file://. No server, no account, no CDN. KaTeX is vendored locally, and every lab draws from numbers computed in your browser.

Read the badge first

Exact means the numbers are computed live. Illustrative means a stored or schematic illustration — the BERT mask table and the β-VAE toy density are labelled that way.

Notation is fixed

\(\eta\) is the learning rate, \(\lambda\) a penalty strength, \(D\) a feature width and \(\mathbf z\) a latent vector. Logits are pre-softmax scores, everywhere in M0–M6.
