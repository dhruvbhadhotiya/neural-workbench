How to use this module

offline · exact labs · read in order

Double-click a lesson

Everything runs from file://. No server, no account, no CDN. KaTeX is vendored locally and every visual is drawn from numbers computed in your browser.

Read the badge first

Exact math means the numbers are computed live. Illustrative means schematic — and says so. All seven M0 labs are exact.

Notation is fixed

\(\eta\) is the learning rate, \(\lambda\) the decay strength, images are NCHW, and logits are pre-softmax outputs. M1–M6 use exactly the same symbols.
