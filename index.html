How to use this site

offline · no account · no build step

Double-click index.html

Everything runs from file://. No server, no network, no CDN. KaTeX is vendored locally; all visuals are drawn on canvas from data you can see.

Read the badge first

Exact math means the numbers are computed in your browser. Illustrative means schematic or precomputed — and says so.

Order matters

M0 sets the notation used everywhere else. After that, M1→M5 follow the five days; M6 is a reference toolkit you can jump into at any point.
